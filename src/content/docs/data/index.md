title: Data sources
description: Official data providers and practical measurement guidance.
---

- [Productivity calculations and historical inputs](/productivity/slowdown/#a-reproducible-historical-comparison): real output, total hours, dated growth comparisons and downloadable inputs.
- [Recent fertility, GDP, and working-hours graphs](/data/recent-fertility-hours/): World Bank and ILOSTAT estimates through 2024, coverage tables, and downloads.
- [Fertility, GDP, and working-hours graphs](/data/fertility-charts/): same-year country paths, sources on every figure, and downloadable data.
- [Fertility data and definitions](/data/fertility/): birth rates, period/cohort fertility, intentions, GDP, and parental time-use sources.

- [Hours worked around the world](/data/working-hours/): a 66-economy 2019 table, historical comparisons, downloadable data, and source links.
- [Leisure and productivity data and graphs](/data/leisure-productivity/): output, hours, earnings, recovery, and welfare inputs.
- [Leisure data and graphs](/data/leisure/): ATUS, AHTUS, MTUS, HETUS, OECD, national surveys, and historical reconstruction.
- [Trade and GDP data and graphs](/data/trade/): WDI, Penn World Table, Maddison, Comtrade, WITS/TRAINS, liberalisation dates, and historical records.

The guides describe coverage, appropriate uses, access considerations, and limitations. They distinguish sources useful for new research from the exact historical inputs used by a paper.
