## Dataset entry format

- **Name and provider:** Official dataset title and responsible organisation.
- **Source link:** Official catalogue, download, or API documentation.
- **Version:** Release date, retrieval date, and persistent identifier if available.
- **Coverage:** Geography, years, observation frequency, and unit of analysis.
- **Variables:** Measures used in the research, including definitions and units.
- **Access and licence:** Public/restricted status, cost, registration, and reuse conditions.
- **Preparation:** Filters, transformations, joins, and known breaks in series.
- **Used by:** Links to relevant paper and finding pages.

Never commit credentials, restricted microdata, or files whose redistribution is not permitted.
